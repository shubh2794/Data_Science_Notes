Differential Privacy

A mathematical guarantee of privacy: the output of an analysis should barely change whether or not any single individual's data is included. Achieved by injecting carefully calibrated noise — trading a little accuracy for a provable bound on what anyone can learn about you.

Data Engineering Privacy Governance

01 Why anonymization isn't enough

  • Removing names doesn't protect privacy — individuals are routinely re-identified by combining "anonymized" datasets with side information.
  • Differential privacy gives a formal, composable guarantee instead of ad-hoc masking.
  • The promise: your presence in the dataset can't be confidently detected from the output.

02 The ε guarantee

A mechanism is ε-differentially private if adding or removing one person changes the output distribution by at most a factor of e^ε. Smaller ε = more privacy = more noise. Drag it.

03 How it's done

  • Sensitivity: how much one record can change the answer (for a count, it's 1).
  • Laplace / Gaussian mechanism: add noise scaled to sensitivity / ε — bigger sensitivity or smaller ε ⇒ more noise.
  • The noise is what makes any single individual's contribution statistically deniable.

04 The privacy budget

  • Each query spends some ε; under composition, repeated queries add up and erode the guarantee.
  • So ε is a finite budget allocated across all analyses — you can't query forever for free.
  • Smaller ε (≈0.1–1) is strong privacy; larger ε (≫1) is weak.

05 Where it's used

  • DP-SGD: clip and add noise to gradients so a trained model doesn't memorize individuals.
  • Private analytics: the US Census and big tech use DP for aggregate statistics.
  • Pairs naturally with Federated Learning — decentralized data plus DP for strong privacy. See also Data Quality & Governance.

06 Takeaways

  • DP = a formal privacy guarantee: one person's data barely changes the output.
  • Add noise scaled to sensitivity/ε — smaller ε means more privacy and more noise.
  • ε is a budget spent across queries (composition).
  • Powers DP-SGD, census statistics, and pairs with Federated Learning.
