The notes, in 3 parts

Three parts. Part 1 solves Ax = b by hand: row reduction, echelon forms, Gaussian elimination and the rank test for when a solution exists. Part 2 is the theory of undoing a square matrix: the determinant from its defining properties, then the inverse, one-sided inverses, the pseudoinverse and the Penrose family. Part 3 is how systems are really solved on a computer: LU, Cholesky and QR, and the condition number that says how many digits survive.
