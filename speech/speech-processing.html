Speech Processing

The audio modality: turning speech into text (recognition) and text into speech (synthesis), plus the representations in between. Modern systems are end-to-end neural models — and increasingly fold into multimodal LLMs.

Speech & Audio ASR · TTS Multimodal

01 The two core tasks

  • ASR (automatic speech recognition): audio → text.
  • TTS (text-to-speech): text → audio.
  • Both hinge on a good intermediate representation of sound — usually a spectrogram.

02 From waveform to features

Raw audio is a 1-D waveform; models work on a time-frequency picture (spectrogram). Flip the direction to see ASR vs TTS.

Audio ↔ spectrogram ↔ text ASR reads left→right; TTS generates right→left

A mel-spectrogram slices the signal into short frames and shows energy per frequency band over time — perceptually weighted to match human hearing.

03 Speech recognition

  • Old pipeline: acoustic model + pronunciation lexicon + language model, stitched together.
  • Modern end-to-end: a single neural net maps audio frames to text via CTC (alignment-free) or attention-based seq2seq.
  • Whisper-style Transformers trained on huge weakly-labeled audio are robust and multilingual.

04 Speech synthesis

  • Two stages: text → spectrogram (e.g. Tacotron/FastSpeech), then spectrogram → waveform via a neural vocoder (WaveNet, HiFi-GAN).
  • Goal: natural prosody, intonation, and speaker identity (voice cloning).

05 Audio meets LLMs

  • Discrete audio tokens (from neural codecs) let Transformers model speech like text — enabling generative audio and speech LLMs.
  • Multimodal models take speech in and speak back, for real-time voice assistants.
  • Shares the Transformer backbone and tokenization ideas with text and vision.

06 Takeaways

  • ASR (audio→text) and TTS (text→audio) are the two core tasks, bridged by the spectrogram.
  • Modern ASR is end-to-end (CTC / seq2seq, Whisper); TTS is spectrogram + neural vocoder.
  • Discrete audio tokens fold speech into Transformer/LLM modeling.
  • Builds on Transformers; joins text & vision in multimodal models.
